Our Story & Management

The Barbers At Number One opened in May 2010 as Fair Oak's first dedicated barbershop in over 50 years. Led by Lisa Smith and her talented team, we focus on consistent, top-quality cuts, welcoming hospitality, and keeping the village looking sharp.

Experienced Barbers

Our skilled barbers (Lisa, Lauren, Kerri, Kate, Julie, Jo) specialize in both classic scissor cuts and modern precision fades.

Premium Styling Products

We stock trusted salon-grade products including Osmo, Hi Tech Hold Gel, Black & White, and Silhouette.

Community First
