Turn-by-Turn Travel Directions

From Eastleigh

Head towards Fair Oak along Eastleigh Road. Turn left at the traffic lights at the end of Eastleigh Road onto Botley Road. The Barbers At Number One is located approx. 50 yards on the left-hand side.

From Winchester / Twyford

Follow Winchester Road through Twyford heading towards Fair Oak. As you pass the Tesco Express garage on your right, the shop is located 50 yards further on the right (adjacent to The George Pub).

From Botley / Hedge End

Head along Botley Road towards Fair Oak square. Proceed straight through the traffic lights (Eastleigh Road junction) and the barbershop is located 50 yards ahead on the left.

From Lower Upham / Bishops Waltham

Head along Winchester Road and turn left at The Alma Inn into Mortimers Lane. At the bottom of Mortimers Lane turn left onto Winchester Road; pass Tesco and the shop is 50 yards on your right.
