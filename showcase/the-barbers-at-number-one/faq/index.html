Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting The Barbers At Number One.

Do I need to book an appointment?

No! We operate strictly on a sit-and-wait walk-in basis (first come, first served). Simply turn up when it suits you and take a seat.

Do you accept debit or credit cards?

We are a cash-only barbershop. If you do not have cash with you, there is a convenient 24/7 ATM cash machine just 50 yards down the road at the Tesco Express petrol garage.

What are your opening hours?

We are open Monday to Friday from 9:00am – 5:30pm, and Saturday from 8:30am – 2:00pm (closed Sundays). Please note that our last haircut is approximately 30 minutes before closing time.

Do you cut women's or girls' hair?

We are a dedicated men's and boys' barbershop catering to gents and youths of all ages.

When do OAP / Senior Citizen discount rates apply?

Our OAP rate (£11.50 for standard cut, £15.50 for wash & cut) applies to clients aged 65 and over, Monday through Friday only.

How can I check which days a specific barber is working?

You can check our full staff schedule on the Team page, or join our WhatsApp Community for real-time rotas and holiday coverage announcements.

Where are you located and where can I park?

We are located at 1 Botley Road, Fair Oak (SO50 7AN), right next to The George Pub. Nearby short-stay on-street parking and village parking bays are available within easy walking distance.
