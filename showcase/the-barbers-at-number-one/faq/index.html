Quick Answers & Advice

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting The Barbers At Number One.

Do I need to book an appointment?

No! We operate strictly on a sit-and-wait walk-in basis (first come, first served). Simply turn up when it suits you, take a seat, and we will look after you.

Do you accept debit or credit cards?

We are a cash-only barbershop. If you don't have cash with you, there is a convenient 24/7 ATM cash machine just 50 yards away at the Tesco Express petrol garage.

What are your opening hours?

We are open Monday to Friday from 9:00am – 5:30pm, and Saturday from 8:30am – 2:00pm (closed Sundays). Please note that our last haircut is approximately 30 minutes before closing time.

Do you cut women's or girls' hair?

We are a dedicated men's and boys' barbershop catering to gents and youths of all ages.

When do OAP / Senior Citizen discount rates apply?

Our OAP rate (£11.50 standard cut, £15.50 wash & cut) applies to clients aged 65 and over, Monday through Friday only.

How can I check which days a specific barber is working?

You can check our full staff rota on our Team page, or join our WhatsApp Community for live shift updates.

Where are you located and where can I park?

We are located at 1 Botley Road, Fair Oak (SO50 7AN), right next to The George Pub. Free village parking bays and on-street parking are available within a 1-minute walk.
